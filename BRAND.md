# VICUT: brand

**VICUT** ("VI cut") is an AI cut room for GTA 6 streams. It watches the biggest GTA 6 streams live, reads chat and cuts
the moments everyone is talking about, minutes after they happen. It runs on the cutroom.fun clipping engine but has its
own name, site and handles.

- **Name:** VICUT. Written VICUT in display type; "VICUT" or "Vicut" in running text. It reads as "VI cut" (GTA VI) with a
  hint of Vice.
- **Tagline:** GTA 6 streams, clipped live.
- **Second line:** Every five-star moment, first.
- **Domain:** **vicut.fun**. On 2026-11-18 its registry (RDAP) showed vicut.fun, vicut.tv and vicut.live unregistered.
  vicuts.com is taken.
- **X:** **@vicutfun**. X's own availability check on 2026-11-18 said @vicutfun, @vicutclips, @getvicut and @vicuttv were
  available; @vicut is taken. Use the same handle on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube where it's free.

## Rules (trademarks)

- Never use "GTA", "Grand Theft Auto", "Rockstar" or "Leonida" in the **name, domain, handle or logo**. Take-Two
  enforces them, and Leonida is the user's other project (LEONIDA ONLINE).
- Describing the content is fine ("clips of GTA 6 streams", "GTA 6 streams, clipped live").
- No Rockstar logos, key art, or the Pricedown typeface.
- Every page carries the "fan project, not affiliated" line.
- No coins, tickers or contract addresses on VICUT. It's a clipping brand, not a launchpad.

## Look

- **Mark:** a wanted-level star sliced in two by a teal cut line, on a night-navy tile. Files: `assets/mark.svg`,
  `favicon.svg` (pure shapes, no font).
- **Wordmark:** "VI" in the sunset gradient, "CUT" in cream, in Anton.
- **Palette:** a night drive into a sunset.

  | token | hex | use |
  |---|---|---|
  | ink | `#0b0a16` | background |
  | panel | `#16142a` | cards |
  | text | `#fff4ec` | cream text |
  | sun-1 / sun-2 / sun-3 | `#ffb347` / `#ff5e7e` / `#d63af9` | the sunset gradient (headlines, the mark, primary buttons) |
  | cut | `#23f0d6` | the cut line, eyebrows, the "ready m:ss after" tags |

- **Type:** Anton (headlines, uppercase), Inter (text), JetBrains Mono (numbers, labels).
- **Motifs:** a striped sunset and a neon grid road (the hero, the banner, the share image).

## Voice

Short, present tense, about the moment, never about us. Say what happened and how fast the clip was ready. No hype words,
no "🚀", and at most one emoji per post.
