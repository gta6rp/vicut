How it works

From the stream to your feed in minutes.

  1. 01

    Watch

    It tunes in to the stream itself, in 1080p, and stays on air for the whole session.

  2. 02

    Listen and read

    Every word is transcribed as it's said, and chat's pace is tracked second by second.

  3. 03

    The call

    When chat loses it, an editor model watches the moment, scores it and writes the hook. Most moments get dropped.

  4. 04

    Cut and post

    The keepers are framed for a phone (cam on top, gameplay below), captioned and posted while the moment is still fresh.
