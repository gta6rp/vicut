      How a live cut works

      Four stages. Minutes, not days.

      1. 01

        Found

        The room scores every second against this streamer's own normal: loudness, chat speed, laughs, “clip it”. A spike in all of them at once is a moment.

      2. 02

        Editor

        An editor model reads the minute around it, looks at the frames, and drops what needs context or has no payoff. It writes the hook from what was actually said.

      3. 03

        Cut

        The keeper is framed for a phone (cam on top, gameplay below), captioned word by word, and rendered while the stream keeps going.

      4. 04

        Ready

        The finished clip lands in the cut list and on the clip wall, with how long after the moment it was ready.
